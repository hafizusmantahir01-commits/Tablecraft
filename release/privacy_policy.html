Tablecraft Privacy Policy

Innovexa Technologies · Last updated November 22, 2026

Summary

Tablecraft is an offline jigsaw puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

What is stored on your device

To remember your game, Tablecraft saves a few things on your device only: your level progress and stars, daily puzzles, coins, best times, achievements, settings, an unfinished puzzle, and the name and profile picture you choose. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Your photos

When you use Create or change your profile picture, Tablecraft opens your photo gallery or camera only when you ask it to, and only uses the one photo you pick or take. On Android and iOS a copy of that photo is kept inside the app so you can continue the puzzle later. On the web the photo stays in memory and is gone when you close the page. Photos are never uploaded.

Internet and permissions

Tablecraft does not need an internet connection to play. The only permission it may ask for is access to your photos or camera, and only when you pick or take a photo yourself.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything Tablecraft has saved at any time: open Me, then Settings, then Reset all game data. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data.

Children

Tablecraft is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
